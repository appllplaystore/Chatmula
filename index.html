About this app

ChatMula brings local language teachers and international learners together. Practise Swahili, English and other languages through friendly conversations, learn everyday expressions and cultural context, and offer language help to eligible learners. Local speakers may earn from eligible teaching sessions; earnings are subject to the app’s terms and availability. Some features require an internet connection and account activation.
